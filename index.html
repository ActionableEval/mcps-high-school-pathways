03 / UNDERSTAND RANKING

Rank your preferences

Rank only programs at schools you are truly interested in and would attend if offered. List up to eight in your preferred order; you do not need to fill all eight. [2]

EXAMPLE

Zinnia’s ranking

  1. 1
    Science, Math & Computer ScienceFirst choice
  2. 2
    CybersecuritySecond choice
  3. 3
    EngineeringThird choice

All three are in Zinnia’s region. She qualifies and would attend any of them.

IN THIS EXAMPLE

First-choice students fill all seats

If first-choice students fill all 60 out-of-area seats, no seats remain for second-choice students and no further drawing can offer them a seat unless a seat opens.

This is a capacity example, not a confirmed MCPS first-choice-first rule.

Lower choices can offer another option.

If choice 1 is unavailable, Zinnia may receive a lower choice. Ranking first does not guarantee a seat.

A SEPARATE PATHWAY

Automatic review

MCPS automatically reviews eighth graders for IB and Science, Math & Computer Science, separately from the lottery.

No application is needed for central review. Still apply and rank a program you want for lottery consideration. [2, 3]

AUTOMATIC-PLACEMENT BENCHMARKS

Science, Math & Computer Science

99th percentile or higher in math and 3.8 GPA or higher in Grade 7 and Grade 8 math and science grades.

International Baccalaureate (IB)

95th percentile or higher in reading or math and 3.75 GPA or higher in Grade 7 and Grade 8 English, social studies, math, and science grades.

Meeting these criteria qualifies for an automatic seat. Local or regional percentiles and prerequisites apply; committees may review other evidence. [3]

How many seats are actually available?

Actual seat numbers are not confirmed here. Each example uses 60 out-of-area seats + 60 additional home-school Center seats. MCPS’s published figures are preliminary and may vary by school. Check with MCPS for final capacity. [2]
